ASKED

What makes SoundAngels different from other headphones?

SoundAngels was built around one idea. Sound should be big and honest, comfort should last all day, and the build should hold up for years. That means a real forty millimeter angled driver, memory foam cushions instead of thin pads, a cast aluminium hinge instead of a stamped one, and active noise cancellation that actually works.

Are SoundAngels wireless?

Yes. SoundAngels connect over Bluetooth with a stable low latency link, and still include a wired option for when you want zero compression.

How long does the battery last?

Thirty hours with active noise cancellation on, and forty hours with it off. Ten minutes on the charger gets you three hours of playback.

What is the driver size?

A forty millimeter dynamic driver, angled eighteen degrees toward the ear canal for direct acoustic resolution instead of a flat, off axis response.

Do the finishes affect the sound?

No. Every colorway shares the exact same driver, hinge, and foam. The finish only changes the shell color, never the acoustics.

Can I use them for calls?

Yes. A triple microphone array picks up your voice and filters out background noise, so calls stay clear even outside.

Do they fold for travel?

Yes. The cast aluminium hinge folds flat and is rated for twenty five thousand cycles, so it holds up to daily packing without loosening.

What is in the box?

The headphones, a braided charging cable, a wired audio cable, and a travel pouch.

How do I keep them in good shape?

Store your headphones in the included protective case when traveling. Clean the ear cushions and headband using a dry, lint-free microfiber cloth. Avoid exposure to extreme heat, moisture, or harsh cleaning solvents to preserve the leatherette seals and aerospace alloy hinges.
